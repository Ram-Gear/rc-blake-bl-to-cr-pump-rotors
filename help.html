Storyboard

Example: job B610197, Worthington lube oil pump (the spreadsheet's own values).

  1. Intro: job B610197 (0:01) - This is R.C. Blake's backlash to comparator readings program for pump rotors, using the sheet's own example: job B610197, the Worthington lube oil pump.
  2. Rotor basics (0:14) - Enter the rotor basics: 10 teeth, 1.5 transverse diametral pitch, a 20 degree pressure angle and a 30 degree helix angle.
  3. Extended centers and OD (0:24) - These rotors run on extended centers. Enter the engaging center distance, 7.58, and the outside diameter, 8.905.
  4. Set backlash (0:35) - Now the set backlash from the specification: 0.006 minimum and 0.012 maximum.
  5. Comparator readings (0:44) - Here are the comparator readings: minus 0.030135 at max backlash and minus 0.034894 at min. Minus means thick; plus or no sign means thin.
  6. Chordal and span checks (0:59) - Below are the chordal addendum, 0.69485, and chordal thickness, 0.98924 max and 0.98597 min. Span over 4 teeth is 6.51018 max and 6.50757 min.
  7. Help (1:17) - Need a refresher on the terms? Help explains every input and plays this video again.

This tool is provided in good faith for general information only. No warranty is made as to its accuracy or completeness. Use at your own risk and verify any results before relying on them.

Inputs and terms

NRNumber of teeth on the rotor
DPTTransverse diametral pitch
PATTransverse pressure angle
HAHelix angle
CEEngaging (extended) center distance
ODOutside diameter of rotor
MIN / MAX SET BLSpecified set backlash range
CStandard centers = (NP+NR)/(2*DPT)
RCRatio of centers = CE / C
CRComparator reading. ( + ) or no sign = thin, ( - ) = thick
% SET BLShare of set backlash applied to the pinion (hidden row 51)

Yellow cells are inputs. Hover any result in the program to see the original Excel formula. Rows that were hidden in Roger Blake's sheet are under "Hidden working calculations".
